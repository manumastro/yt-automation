# Hook Playbook — Finest Explainer

Catalogo completo degli hook framework identificati nel canale, con esempi reali e varianti applicabili.

---

## Framework 1: Cold Open Narrativo

**Pattern:** Entra direttamente nella scena dell'evento. Nessun "benvenuto", nessun contesto preliminare. Il primo frame è già azione.

**Formula:**  
`[Proper Noun]. In [year/month], [dramatic action] began. [Sensory detail 1], [sensory detail 2], and [sensory detail 3].`

### Esempi dal canale

1. **WannaCry (viruses pt1, 3.2M views):**  
   *"In May 2017, computers around the world began locking up without warning. Screens went black, files were encrypted, and a red message appeared in their place."*

2. **MyDoom (viruses pt1):**  
   *"In early 2004, My Doom appeared and quickly became one of the fastest spreading computer worms ever recorded."*

3. **Stuxnet (viruses pt2):**  
   *"In 2010, a computer virus called Stuxnet was discovered by cybersecurity researchers. At first, it looked like another complex piece of malware, but deeper analysis revealed something far more sophisticated."*

4. **Kuchisake-Onna (japanese legends):**  
   *"In 1979, the story of a mysterious figure called Kuchisake Ona, the slitmouthed woman, spread rapidly across Japan."*

### Varianti suggerite
- **Per tech/business:** *"In [year], [number] [users/companies/systems] went dark simultaneously. No warning. No explanation."*
- **Per health/science:** *"In [year], doctors in [location] noticed something they'd never seen before. [Alarming symptom]."*
- **Per history:** *"[Year]. [Location]. [Unexpected detail about a mundane setting]. Then everything changed."*

**Quando funziona meglio:** Argomenti con un evento scatenante chiaro, data precisa, conseguenze drammatiche. Storia, tecnologia, disastri.

## Framework 2: Entità Misteriosa con Descrizione Fisica

**Pattern:** Nome dell'entità come apertura, seguito da una descrizione fisica o contestuale che crea disagio o curiosità.

**Formula:**  
`[Name]. [Year/Context]. [Physical description that feels "wrong"]. [Why it's unsettling].`

### Esempi dal canale

1. **This Man (internet mysteries):**  
   *"Beginning in 2006, a psychiatrist claimed that multiple patients, all strangers to each other, described seeing the exact same man in their dreams, resulting in a composite sketch of an ordinary yet deeply unsettling face. Thick eyebrows, a receding hairline, and an expression that felt wrong in a way people couldn't explain."*

2. **Smile Dog (genz trauma):**  
   *"The image shows a husky staring directly at the camera with an unnaturally wide human-like grin."*

3. **Hachishaku-sama (japanese legends):**  
   *"The figure was unnaturally tall, around 8ft, and seemed oddly thin and disproportionate. It wore a wide-brimmed hat and long black hair hung down from beneath it."*

4. **Jinmen Ken (japanese legends):**  
   *"A creature said to have the body of a dog and the face of a middle-aged man... The body was a dog's, but the face was unmistakably that of a middle-aged man. The expression was tired, irritated, and strangely human."*

### Varianti suggerite
- **Per AI/tech:** *"[Name]. A system that [benign function]. But users started reporting [alarming behavior]."*
- **Per business:** *"[Company]. On paper, it looked like [normal thing]. Behind the scenes, [disturbing reality]."*
- **Per nature:** *"[Species]. From a distance, it looks [normal]. Up close, [unsettling detail]."*

**Quando funziona meglio:** Argomenti horror, soprannaturali, creepy, creature, figure misteriose. Funziona perché la descrizione fisica crea un'immagine mentale impossibile da ignorare.

## Framework 3: Dissonanza Visiva/Contestuale

**Pattern:** Presenta qualcosa che sembra innocuo, poi rivela che è tutt'altro. Sfrutta il contrasto tra aspettativa e realtà.

**Formula:**  
`[Innocent-looking thing]. At first glance, [it seemed harmless/normal]. But [reality].`

### Esempi dal canale

1. **Happy Tree Friends (genz trauma):**  
   *"At first glance, it looked like a harmless children's cartoon. The characters were colorful forest animals, and the animation style was bright and simple. However, the series quickly became known for doing the opposite of what its visual style suggested."*

2. **Bonzi Buddy (viruses pt1):**  
   *"At first glance, it looked like a harmless desktop companion designed to help users browse the internet, tell jokes, and manage basic tasks... But behind the friendly exterior, Bonzi Buddy was far from innocent."*

3. **I Love You virus (viruses pt1):**  
   *"That message carried the subject line I love you and included an attachment named love letter for you.txt, a visual basic script disguised as a harmless text file."*

4. **Obey the Walrus (creepy youtube):**  
   *"At first glance... the video quality is poor, which makes everything feel even more uncomfortable."* → real person with polio, not supernatural

### Varianti suggerite
- **Per food/consumer:** *"[Product]. It promised [benefit]. What it actually did was [surprising reality]."*
- **Per apps/tech:** *"[App]. Millions downloaded it for [function]. It was quietly [hidden behavior]."*
- **Per people:** *"[Person] seemed [positive trait]. Behind closed doors, [revelation]."*

**Quando funziona meglio:** Argomenti dove la verità è opposta all'apparenza — spyware mascherato da tool, virus mascherati da amore, cartoni violenti.

## Framework 4: Scenario Ipotesi Personale

**Pattern:** Pone lo spettatore come protagonista di uno scenario impossibile. Usa seconda persona ("you").

**Formula:**  
`[Context]. Now, imagine this. You [impossible action]. [Immediate consequence].`

### Esempi dal canale

1. **Mercury (planet survival):**  
   *"Now, imagine this. You suddenly teleport to the surface of Mercury with no time to prepare. If you appear on the sunlit side of the planet, you would be killed almost instantly by extreme heat."*

2. **Venus (planet survival):**  
   *"But the moment you arrive, that illusion disappears. In less than a second, you are exposed to atmospheric pressure equal to being 900m underwater on Earth."*

3. **Jupiter (planet survival):**  
   *"The moment a human appears inside Jupiter's atmosphere, powerful radiation floods the body. Cells suffer catastrophic DNA damage and the nervous system shuts down almost immediately."*

### Varianti suggerite
- **Per survival:** *"Imagine you wake up in [location]. You have [no resources]. [Immediate threat]. What do you do?"*
- **Per history:** *"It's [year]. You're a [role] in [location]. [Historical event] is about to happen."*
- **Per body/science:** *"Your [body part] suddenly [abnormal event]. Within [time], [consequence]."*

**Quando funziona meglio:** Argomenti scientifici, survival, what-if, body horror. ⚠️ ATTENZIONE: questo è l'unico framework che nel canale ha PERFORMATO MALE (5K views vs 600K+). Probabilmente perché manca il fattore "creepy/misterioso" che attira il target del canale.

## Framework 5: Forum/Post Virale Apertura

**Pattern:** Inizia con la storia di un post online, un forum, un'interazione digitale. Sfrutta il realismo della piattaforma.

**Formula:**  
`[Name]. [Year]. A [post/comment/video] appeared on [platform]. [First line of the post or description of the content].`

### Esempi dal canale

1. **Kisaragi Station (japanese legends):**  
   *"One day, a post appeared on a famous Japanese internet forum called 2ch. It began with a simple line. 'This might just be my imagination, but is it okay if I ask something?' The user who wrote it went by the name Hasumi."*

2. **Cicada 3301 (internet mysteries):**  
   *"In early 2012, an unusual message appeared on 4chan. It was simple, almost plain, but it carried a strange promise."*

3. **Ben Drowned (internet mysteries):**  
   *"In 2010, a story began appearing online under the name Jadusable. The writer claimed he had bought a used copy of The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask, already containing a save file named Ben."*

4. **Chipchan (internet mysteries):**  
   *"The legend of Chipchan began on 4chan and various paranormal forums. She claimed that in 1999, a corrupt police officer she referred to only as P had implanted a device called Verachip into her body."*

### Varianti suggerite
- **Per Reddit stories:** *"A user on r/[subreddit] posted a question that seemed ordinary. The responses were anything but."*
- **Per Twitter/X:** *"[Year]. A single tweet from [account] set off a chain reaction nobody predicted."*
- **Per YouTube:** *"In [year], a video was uploaded with no title, no description, and no context. It went viral anyway."*

**Quando funziona meglio:** Creepypasta, 2ch/4chan stories, internet mysteries, ARG, viral phenomena. Funziona perché il formato "post online" è immediatamente credibile e relatable per il target.
