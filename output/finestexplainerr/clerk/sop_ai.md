# CHANNEL REPLICATION SOP: Finest Explainer

## CHANNEL PROFILE
- **Name:** Finest Explainer
- **Niche:** Faceless explainer — creepy, mysterious, tech & science topics
- **Format:** Long form, ~8 min, voiceover only
- **Avg views:** ~681K
- **Video count:** 7
- **Style:** Documentary narrator, no face, no CTA, pure content
- **Top video:** 3.2M views (Computer Viruses Pt.1)
- **Failure case:** 5K views (Planet Survival — off-niche)

## SCRIPT FORMULA

### Structure
Each video is a **LISTICLE** of 5-8 items. Each item follows:

```
1. NAME + CONTEXT (3-5 sec)
   "Name of thing. In [year], [brief setup]."
   
2. WHAT IT IS / WHAT HAPPENED (15-30 sec)
   Concrete description. Use sensory details.
   "Screens went black, files were encrypted..."
   
3. HOW IT WORKED / ESCALATION (20-40 sec)
   Mechanism, spread, or progression.
   Use "within hours/days" for urgency.
   
4. IMPACT / CONSEQUENCES (10-20 sec)
   Numbers, scope, human cost.
   "hundreds of thousands across 150 countries"
   
5. RESOLUTION / TWIST (10-15 sec)
   How it ended. Or: it never ended.
   "The outbreak slowed only after..."
   
6. TRANSITION → next item (0-5 sec)
   Usually no transition. Jump cut to next.
```

**Total per item:** 60-90 seconds  
**Total items per video:** 5-8  
**Total video length:** ~8 minutes

### Hook Templates
1. COLD_OPEN: "[Name]. In [year], [dramatic event] began. [2 concrete details]."
2. IDENTIFICATION: "[Demographic] [topic] Explained In [X] Minutes" → Hook: "[Recognizable reference from that era]"
3. ENTITY_MYSTERY: "[Name]. [Year]. [Forum/platform]. A [user/post] appeared with [odd detail]."
4. HYPOTHESIS: "[Planet/concept]. Now, imagine this. You [impossible scenario] with no time to prepare."

### Retention Rules
- **MUST:** Start every item with a proper noun (name of virus/person/place)
- **MUST:** Include at least 1 specificity spike per item (date, number, name)
- **MUST:** Each item must have a mini-climax (worst moment, twist, or impact)
- **MUST:** Vary tone between items (serious → absurd → dark → funny)
- **MUST:** Use "within hours/days" or countdown language for urgency
- **AVOID:** Any intro beyond the first item name (no "let me tell you about...")
- **AVOID:** Personal opinions or commentary — stay factual-omniscient
- **AVOID:** Filler phrases ("So basically", "What happened was", "And then")
- **AVOID:** Breaking the fourth wall (no "you might be wondering")

### Tone & Voice
- **Tone:** Detached documentary narrator — omniscient, clinical, slightly ominous
- **Vocabulary:** Accessible but precise. Use technical terms but immediately explain them.
- **Complexity:** Middle school reading level for comprehension, PhD-level for specificity
- **Sentence structure:** Short declarative sentences. Fragment for emphasis. Rarely complex.
- **Emotional register:** Low affect, high content. Let the facts create the emotion.

### Content Pillars
1. **Cyber Threats** — Viruses, malware, ransomware, hacks (~30%)
2. **Internet Horror** — Creepypasta, urban legends, disturbing content (~45%)
3. **Japanese Culture/Horror** — 2ch stories, yōkai, urban legends (~15%)
4. **Science What-If** — Hypothetical scenarios, planetary science (~10%)
⚠️ Pillar 4 significantly underperforms — stick to 1-3 for best results.

**Time anchor:** Always "In X Minutes" (7-9 min sweet spot)

### Example 1: Computer Viruses Pt.1 (3.2M views)
- **Items:** WannaCry → You Are An Idiot → I Love You → MyDoom → Bonzi Buddy → Petya → Memz
- **Pattern:** Serious → Absurd → Serious → Serious → Semi-funny → Dark → Chaotic

### Example 2: Gen Z Childhood Trauma (657K views)
- **Items:** Herobrine → Happy Tree Friends → Lavender Town → Little Babies Ice Cream → Smile Dog → Talking Angela → Slenderman
- **Pattern:** Gaming → Cartoon → Gaming → Commercial → Creepypasta → App → Creepypasta
- **Key hook:** Direct address to generation. Opens with most recognizable reference (Herobrine/Minecraft).

### Example 3: Planet Survival (5K views — LOW PERFORMER)
- **Items:** Mercury → Venus → Mars → Jupiter → Saturn → Uranus → Neptune
- **Pattern:** Sequential by distance from sun — predictable
- **Key hook:** "You suddenly teleport to the surface of Mercury"
- **Why it failed:** No variety in structure, no "twist" items, purely sequential, no cultural hook
