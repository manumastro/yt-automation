## Mini research recap — verified facts & source points

1. **The underground bunker history goes back to Franklin D. Roosevelt in 1942**, after the US entered World War II.
   - Source: PBS / AP — https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/a-brief-history-of-the-underground-white-house-bunker-at-the-heart-of-trumps-ballroom-legal-case

2. **The bunker beneath the East Wing included the Presidential Emergency Operations Center (PEOC)**, a command facility for the president and staff.
   - Source: PBS / AP

3. **The PEOC became widely known after 9/11**, when Vice President Dick Cheney was rushed there during the attacks.
   - Source: PBS / AP

4. **The East Wing was demolished in October 2025** to make way for the new project.
   - Source: BBC — https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/clyx9xyy0j9o

5. **The ballroom project is described as a 90,000-square-foot / roughly 8,400-square-meter development** with capacity reported up to **1,350 guests**.
   - Source: PBS / AP; BBC

6. **The White House and Trump publicly framed the underground works as national-security infrastructure**, including bomb shelters, secure telecommunications, secure air handling, biodefense features, and medical facilities.
   - Source: PBS / AP; ABC — https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-04-01/trump-confirms-white-house-underground-bunker-build/106520732

7. **Trump described the ballroom as effectively a “shed” for what was being built underneath**, emphasizing that the underground portion was the bigger story.
   - Source: ABC

8. **Judge Richard Leon halted above-ground construction in April 2026**, arguing Congress must approve the project and warning that national security is not a blank check.
   - Source: BBC — https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c8r48mgnzmjo

9. **An appeals court later allowed all construction to resume pending further review**, keeping the legal battle active.
   - Source: BBC — https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/clyx9xyy0j9o

10. **Taxpayer money would cover security-related portions, while Trump said private donors would fund the ballroom itself**, creating a split public/private framing.
   - Source: PBS / AP; BBC
