## Entry map

### 1) Raven Rock Mountain Complex (Site R)
- Setup: Maryland/Pennsylvania border, Cold War continuity site
- Mechanism: underground command city for DoD
- Spike: ~650+ ft inside mountain, nickname “Underground Pentagon”
- Mini-climax: still operational

### 2) The Greenbrier Bunker
- Setup: luxury resort hiding a secret Congress shelter
- Mechanism: hidden facility beneath hotel
- Spike: exposed publicly in 1992
- Mini-climax: built for lawmakers who never used it

### 3) Cheyenne Mountain Complex
- Setup: NORAD command in Colorado
- Mechanism: blast doors + shock-isolated buildings
- Spike: doors weigh ~25 tons each (reported), hardened military site
- Mini-climax: designed to keep functioning during attack

### 4) Mount Weather Emergency Operations Center
- Setup: FEMA continuity hub in Virginia
- Mechanism: emergency relocation + command infrastructure
- Spike: expanded during/after Cold War, remains key contingency site
- Mini-climax: activation rumors during major crises

### 5) Project Greek Island (West Virginia)
- Setup: secret relocation plan for U.S. Congress
- Mechanism: coded system, media blackout, hidden logistics
- Spike: decades classified operation under a public hotel
- Mini-climax: one magazine investigation ended the secrecy

### 6) Yamantau / Mezhgorye (Russia, reported)
- Setup: remote closed city linked to deep bunker systems
- Mechanism: restricted zone + military rail/tunnel reports
- Spike: outsiders still have limited verified access
- Mini-climax/payoff: some Cold War bunkers became museums; others may still be waiting
